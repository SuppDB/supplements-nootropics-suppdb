Zinc

Chemical determination, molecular structure, and commercial supplement product occurrences.

Zinc appears in 61 commercial supplement products across 57 brands in this SuppDB sample, at per-serving doses ranging 0.162–75 mg. It is catalogued as a mineral ingredient, supplied in forms such as Zinc Citrate, Zinc Oxide, and Zinc Gluconate. Its NIH PubChem record lists molecular formula Zn, a molecular weight of 65.4 g/mol, and InChIKey HCHKCACWOHOZIP-UHFFFAOYSA-N. Label reference values in the sample record a recommended intake of 11 mg and an upper safety limit up to 40 mg. Sold under the Kirkman, MegaFood, and Nature's Bounty labels in this sample.
